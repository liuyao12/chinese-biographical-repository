# Fixed received retained-topic ordinal deaths

Opt-in `normalized_date.event_evidence_binding.kind` is `fixed_received_retained_topic_ordinal_death`. A small trusted index (`scripts/fixed_received_life_date_slots.json`) binds a scope key to one proof path/SHA, chapter and life-event ID. The proof keeps the actual complete received line-unit witness, fixed commit/blob/raw SHA, every paragraph/layer/source position and all raw line fragments. Source metadata is checked in its actual format; no fabricated revision or absolute-position fields are inserted.

The local death event retains only its real paragraph. Date evidence separately retains every paragraph from the explicit era marker to the death, all increasing omitted ordinals and any 明年 step. The existing `relative_ce_regnal_era_span` validator performs the numeric conversion. The original date expression stays 十二年; no month/day, birth estimate or office end is generated.

A zero-offset biography seed and retained local topic resolve current canonical IDs from stable source MIDs. The local topic may be the same death paragraph or the immediately preceding actual paragraph, using existing `prior_subject_valid`; no name is inserted before a subjectless death. Distinct nearby people are enumerated as non-target roles. Every year marker before the target death must be accounted for; later years remain in the full quote but do not date the earlier death.

The old continuous-paragraph length rule and existing death-chain kinds are unchanged. Known trusted slots are checked once at native/catalog load, including required death, at-death age and any explicitly configured origin; removing the new binding cannot silently downgrade a configured event to an unqualified date. Bundle validation is limited to actual events carrying the new kind. There is no global cross-root cache or per-person whole-catalog scan.

The numeric dates and retained-topic ownership are editorial/source-context readings, with human_reviewed false. Age counting remains unspecified_source_age and birth_year null. Origin is biographical_origin, not birthplace, residence or death/burial place.
